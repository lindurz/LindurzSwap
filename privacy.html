Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lindurz ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the Lindurz decentralized exchange platform that allows users to trade digital assets across multiple blockchain networks including Ronin, Berachain, and Monad.

🌐 Important: Lindurz is a non-custodial, decentralized platform. We do not hold, store, or have access to your funds, private keys, or personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of our decentralized services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Non-Personal Information

We may collect non-personal information that does not directly identify you:

  • Blockchain Data: Public blockchain transaction data, wallet addresses (public keys), and smart contract interactions
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you interact with our interface
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device information, and IP address (for security purposes)

2.2 What We Don't Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Private keys or seed phrases
  • Personal identification information
  • Financial information
  • Email addresses (unless voluntarily provided)

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information for:

  • Service Operation: To provide and maintain our decentralized exchange services
  • Security: To protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Analytics: To improve user experience and service performance
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations where required

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

🔗 Important Notice: When you interact with blockchain networks, your transactions are public and permanent on the respective blockchain.

Third parties include:

  • Blockchain networks (Ronin, Berachain, Monad)
  • Wallet providers (MetaMask, Ronin Wallet, etc.)
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Mixpanel)
  • Infrastructure providers (RPC nodes, API services)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information:

  • Encryption of data in transit
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure infrastructure management
🛡️ Your Responsibility: As a non-custodial service, you are responsible for securing your wallet, private keys, and seed phrases. We cannot recover lost credentials.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your information:

  • Access: Request information about what data we have
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@lindurz.xyz

7. International Data Transfers

As a decentralized service operating on global blockchain networks, your data may be processed and stored in various jurisdictions worldwide. By using our services, you consent to this transfer and processing.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on our website
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Providing notice through our platform (if significant changes)

Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@lindurz.xyz

Website: lindurz.xyz

We typically respond to privacy-related inquiries within 7-10 business days.
